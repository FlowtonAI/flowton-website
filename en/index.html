[ FAQ ]

Good questions.

Why is there a waitlist?

Because we set up and support every firm personally. We only take on as many firms as we can look after well. Everyone who signs up hears from us in order, starting with the free check.

What does it cost?

That depends on what gets automated. That's why you get a written fixed price after the free check, before we build anything. And the time guarantee applies: if the automation doesn't save the agreed time, you get your money back.

Is it worth it for a firm of six people?

Especially then. In small firms, the owner often does the office work themselves, in the evening. Move the calculator above to your hours and see what comes out.

We already have a website. Do we need a new one?

Not necessarily. The automations also work with your existing website. In the free check, we'll tell you honestly whether a new one is worth it.

Do we need new software?

No. We build on what you already use: your phone, your email, your accounting software and your quote template.

Does the AI understand Swiss German?

Partly. Speech AI still struggles more with dialect than with Standard German. So the phone assistant confirms key details like the address with the caller, and voice notes for quotes work best in Standard German or English. We'd rather tell you before than after.

What happens to our customers' data?

We follow Swiss data protection law. Before anything goes live, we show you which data goes where. And your customers always know when they're talking to an assistant rather than a person.
